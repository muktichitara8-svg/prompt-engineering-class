Stay Secure Online

Practical tips to protect your accounts — especially if your email appeared in a breach.

Use Unique Passwords

Passwords
  • Never reuse the same password on two different websites.
  • A strong password has 12+ characters, numbers, symbols, and mixed case.
  • Use a password manager like Bitwarden (free & open source).
  • If one site is breached, unique passwords keep your other accounts safe.

Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

2FA
  • 2FA adds a second step to login — like a code sent to your phone.
  • Even with your password stolen, attackers cannot log in without your device.
  • Use an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy) — safer than SMS.
  • Enable 2FA on email, banking, and social media accounts first.

Recognise Phishing Emails

Phishing
  • Phishing pretends to be a trusted company to steal your info.
  • Check the sender's actual email address — not just the display name.
  • Do not click links in unexpected emails. Go to the website directly instead.
  • "Your account will be deleted!" — urgent messages are a classic phishing trick.

If Your Account May Be Exposed

Emergency
  • Step 1: Change your password on that website immediately.
  • Step 2: Change the same password on any other site where you reused it.
  • Step 3: Check your email for unusual login activity.
  • Step 4: Enable 2FA on the affected account right away.
  • Step 5: If banking details were involved, contact your bank.
